Ph.D. work · Rice University

Programmable open quantum systems

How do coherent interactions and the surrounding environment shape quantum dynamics? My doctoral research in Prof. Guido Pagano’s Quantum Simulation Lab at Rice University explored this question using trapped ions.

By engineering both coherent and dissipative interactions on a dual-species ion chain, we investigated excitation transfer dynamics and tunable thermal reservoirs at the interface of quantum information, AMO physics, condensed-matter physics, and chemical physics.

Thermal reservoirs with tunable temperature

Realizing tunable thermal reservoirs in a trapped-ion spin-boson simulator extends the ways we can study open-system dynamics.
