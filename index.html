Building quantum systems to explore complex dynamics and advance quantum computing.

I am an IQIM Postdoctoral Scholar at Caltech, hosted by Manuel Endres’s group, where I work on large-scale quantum computers based on neutral-atom arrays. I received my Ph.D. in Physics from Rice University with Guido Pagano, developing trapped-ion quantum simulators to study open-system chemical dynamics.

My research connects quantum information, AMO physics, and chemical physics through hardware-efficient tools and programmable quantum dynamics.
